Pomodoro break games

Finished a focus block? Here's what to do with the break — a game that ends exactly when your break does, so it never eats into your next Pomodoro.

25 min focus → 5 min break → Word Chain
50 min focus → 10 min break → Word Chain (5 min)

Running a longer break? Play Word Chain and stop with time to spare — we don't have a 10-minute game yet.

We're not a Pomodoro timer

This isn't a productivity app — there's no task list, no focus-session tracker, no dashboard. Keep using whatever timer you already like. When it tells you your break has started, come here, pick a length, and play. When our timer ends, so does the game.
