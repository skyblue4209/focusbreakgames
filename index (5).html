Why not just play any browser game?

Most game sites are built to keep you there — more levels, more tabs, an autoplay queue of "you might also like." This page is built to let you go. Every game here runs on the same clock as your break: when it hits zero, the round ends automatically and you're handed back to your books. There's no "continue playing" button on purpose.

Good for

College classes, grad school reading, board exam prep (NCLEX, MCAT, CPA, LSAT, the bar), coding bootcamps, or any study session where you set a timer and actually want to keep it.
